# How to Register Your Business With CAC in 2026 (Without Getting It Wrong)

> You've decided to make it official. Before you open your laptop and start the CAC registration, there are two things that will trip you up if nobody warns you first — one of them is your NIN, and it's non-negotiable now.

You don't need to queue at a CAC office anymore. That part's actually true — the whole process now runs through CAC's own portal, pre.cac.gov.ng, and your certificate arrives digitally once it's approved. No standing in line, no "come back Monday." But "online" doesn't mean "simple," and there are a couple of details that catch first-timers off guard every week.

![Man reviewing business documents and using his smartphone at a desk](https://bankssolution.ng/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/img/cac-business-registration-nigeria-phone.jpg)

## The one most people don't see coming

Your National Identification Number is now mandatory for CAC registration. Not optional, not "recommended" — mandatory, for every applicant. If you've been putting off getting your NIN, that's the actual first step here, before you even touch the CAC portal. Plenty of people start the registration, get to the identity verification step, and stall out because this is the piece nobody told them about in advance.

## What you actually need before you start

Have these ready before you open the portal: your proposed business name, plus a second choice in case the first one's taken (and it often is — Nigerian business names get reserved fast). The nature of your business, described plainly. Your principal business address. A valid email address and phone number you actually check. And your NIN.

## The process itself

Search the portal to check your name is available, reserve it once it clears, fill in the registration form, upload whatever documents it asks for, pay the statutory fees online, and track your application status until it's approved. It's mostly a formality once your name and NIN are sorted — the friction is almost always earlier in the process, not here.

## Business name or limited liability company?

This is the decision people get wrong most often, usually by overthinking it. Registering a business name is the simpler route — you're a sole proprietor, the paperwork is lighter, and it suits most people just starting out who want to trade legally under a real name instead of "my WhatsApp shop." A limited liability company (Ltd) is a bigger structure: it separates you legally from the business, which matters once you're taking on real risk, bringing in investors, or your liability exposure genuinely needs that wall between you and the company.

If you're not sure which one you need, you probably need a business name for now. Most small operators overestimate how soon they need the Ltd structure, and underestimate how easy it is to register one later once the business has actually grown into needing it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I have to visit a CAC office at any point?

No. The entire process, including document upload, payment, and certificate delivery, happens through the online portal.

### What if my NIN isn't ready yet?

Get it sorted first. It's a mandatory part of registration now, and you can't complete the process without it.

### Can I switch from a business name to a limited liability company later?

Yes — plenty of businesses start as a business name and register as a Ltd once they've actually grown into needing the structure.

### How long does approval usually take?

It varies, but most of the delay tends to happen before submission — getting your NIN sorted and your name approved — rather than in the review itself.
