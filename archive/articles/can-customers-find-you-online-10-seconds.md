# Can Customers Find You Online in Under 10 Seconds? The Question Killing Nigerian Small Businesses

> Open your phone right now. Search your own business name. Count how many seconds it takes to find something useful. If you hesitated even a little, so will your customers — and they won't wait as long as you just did.

Today's customer doesn't call around or ask a neighbor for a recommendation the way they might have a decade ago. They pull out a phone, search, and decide in seconds whether you're worth their time. If nothing useful comes up fast, they move to whoever does show up.

![Nigerian business owner looking shocked while checking his phone](https://bankssolution.ng/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/img/nigerian-business-owner-shocked-phone-search.jpg)

## Run the test right now

Google your business name. What comes up? A working website, an outdated one, or nothing at all? Check your Google Maps listing — is it there, is the information correct, does it have a phone number and hours anyone can trust? Open your WhatsApp Business profile and look at it the way a stranger would: is there a photo, a clear description, a catalog, or is it just a plain number with nothing to signal this is a real business?

## Why ten seconds is the real number

Nobody scrolls to page two anymore. Nobody waits for a slow site to load when three competitors are one tap away. Ten seconds isn't an exaggeration for effect — it's close to the actual window you get before someone decides you're either worth their time or not.

## What failing this test actually costs you

Every customer who searches and finds nothing useful doesn't necessarily conclude you're a bad business. They usually just never find out you existed in the first place — which is quieter, and in some ways worse, than a customer who found you and said no.

## If you just failed this

You're not alone, and this isn't a reason to panic or rebuild everything overnight. It's a reason to fix the specific, fixable gaps — starting with the free ones that don't require a big budget or an ad account.

## Frequently asked questions

### What if my business genuinely doesn't come up when I search it?

That's the exact problem this test is meant to surface — the fix starts with the free, practical steps covered in the companion guide, not a big rebuild.

### Does this apply even if I only sell through Instagram or WhatsApp?

Yes — customers still often search your business name or check your profile completeness before deciding whether to message you.

### Is a Google Maps listing really that important for a small business?

Very — it's often the first thing that appears when someone searches your name, and an incomplete or missing one costs you customers who never even reach out.

### What's the very first thing I should fix?

Whichever of the three checks (Google search, Maps listing, WhatsApp Business profile) came up worst — start with the weakest link, not the one that's easiest to ignore.
