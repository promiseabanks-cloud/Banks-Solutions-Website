# The Nigerian Business Owner's Guide to Getting Found Online (Without Paying for Ads Yet)

> You don't need an ad budget to be findable online. You need the free basics actually done properly — and most Nigerian small businesses skip at least two of them without realizing it.

If you ran the quick test in our last post and didn't like what you found, this is the fix — the free, practical starting point before you ever need to think about paid ads or deeper SEO work.

![Nigerian man using a maps and location app on his smartphone](https://bankssolution.ng/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/img/nigerian-business-owner-google-maps-phone.jpg)

## Google Business Profile, first

This is free, and it's often the single highest-impact thing you can set up. Claim your listing, fill in your real address or service area, your actual hours, your phone number, and photos of your actual premises or products. An incomplete profile — or worse, no profile — means Google has nothing to show a nearby customer searching for exactly what you sell.

## Your WhatsApp Business catalog

Most Nigerian small businesses already use WhatsApp Business, but plenty leave the catalog empty or the profile half-filled. A complete profile — business description, hours, address, and an actual product catalog people can browse — turns WhatsApp from a messaging app into something closer to a real storefront, and it costs nothing extra to set up.

## Reviews aren't just social proof

Customer reviews do double duty: they build trust with a human reading them, and they're a real ranking signal that search engines and map listings actually weigh. Ask satisfied customers directly to leave one — most won't think to unless you ask, not because they're unwilling, but because it simply doesn't occur to them unprompted.

## Keep your name, address, and phone consistent

Your business name, address, and phone number should read identically everywhere — Google, Instagram, WhatsApp, any directory you're listed in. Inconsistency (a slightly different address here, a different phone number there) actively confuses both customers and search engines about which listing is actually correct and current, and it can quietly hurt how findable you are.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to pay for any of this?

No — Google Business Profile, WhatsApp Business, and asking for reviews are all free. This is the starting point before you'd ever consider paid ads.

### How long does it take to set up a Google Business Profile?

The setup itself takes maybe 20-30 minutes; verification can take longer depending on the method Google offers for your business.

### Do reviews really affect whether I show up in search?

Yes — reviews function as both trust signals for customers and ranking signals for the platforms themselves, so they carry more weight than they might seem to.
