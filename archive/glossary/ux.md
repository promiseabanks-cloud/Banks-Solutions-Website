# UX (User Experience)

How easy and clear something is to use, whether people can do what they came to do without friction.

## Why it matters

Good UX is mostly invisible; you only notice the bad kind. On a business website it usually comes down to unglamorous things: can someone find what you do, understand whether it applies to them, and contact you without hunting. Beautiful sites fail this test constantly.
