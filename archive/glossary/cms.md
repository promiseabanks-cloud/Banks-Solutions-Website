# CMS (Content Management System)

The software behind a website that lets you add and edit pages without writing code. WordPress is the most widely used.

## Why it matters

This determines whether you can update your own site or have to pay someone every time a phone number changes. It also determines how easily you can move to another developer later. A site built on an agency's private platform can be very hard to leave.
