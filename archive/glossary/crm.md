# CRM (Customer Relationship Management)

A single system holding every customer, enquiry and conversation, so nothing depends on someone remembering.

## Why it matters

This is the tool that stops enquiries going cold. Without one, leads live in a phone, an inbox and someone's head, and the business cannot answer basic questions like how many enquiries came in last month. A CRM only works if it is built around how you actually operate. Configure your operation around the software instead, and it gets abandoned.
