# Bounce Rate

The share of visitors who arrive on a page and leave without going anywhere else on the site.

## Why it matters

A high bounce rate is a symptom, not a diagnosis. It can mean the page failed to answer the question, or that it answered it completely and the visitor got what they came for. Judge it against what the page was supposed to achieve, not against a benchmark someone quoted you.
