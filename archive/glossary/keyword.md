# Keyword

A word or phrase people type into a search engine when looking for something.

## Why it matters

The useful shift is to think in questions rather than keywords. People increasingly search, and ask AI assistants: in full sentences, so pages built to answer a real question outperform pages stuffed with a phrase. Writing for the phrase rather than the person is now actively counterproductive.
