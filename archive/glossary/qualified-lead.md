# Qualified Lead

An enquiry from someone who genuinely could become a customer: right need, right budget, right timing.

## Why it matters

More enquiries is not automatically better. Fifty unqualified enquiries cost more time than five good ones and produce less revenue. A well-built website filters as much as it attracts, which is why being explicit about who you work with improves results rather than shrinking them.
