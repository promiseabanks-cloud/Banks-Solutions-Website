# E-commerce

Selling products or services directly through your website, including the cart, checkout and payment.

## Why it matters

The build is the easy part. What determines whether it works is payment methods your customers actually use, delivery you can genuinely fulfil, and stock figures that stay accurate. In Nigeria, offering the payment options buyers already trust matters more than any design decision.
