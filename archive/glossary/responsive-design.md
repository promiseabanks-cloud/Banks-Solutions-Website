# Responsive Design

A single website that rearranges itself to suit whatever screen it is viewed on.

## Why it matters

It is the standard approach, but "responsive" only means the layout does not break, not that the experience is good. Genuine mobile design means rethinking navigation, tap targets, image sizes and how much text is reasonable, rather than simply letting things stack.
