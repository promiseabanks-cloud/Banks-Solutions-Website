# Backup

A stored copy of your website files and database that can be restored if something is lost, broken or attacked.

## Why it matters

The important detail is that an untested backup is not a backup. Plenty of businesses discover their backups were failing silently only at the moment they need one. Backups should be automatic, stored somewhere other than your web server, and restored occasionally to prove they work.
