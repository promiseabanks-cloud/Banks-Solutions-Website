# NDPA 2023 (Nigeria Data Protection Act)

Nigeria's data protection law, in force since June 2023. It replaced the older NDPR 2019 and created the Nigeria Data Protection Commission as regulator.

## Why it matters

If you collect names, emails or phone numbers, which every business with a contact form does. This applies to you. Enforcement is real rather than theoretical: the Commission has already issued substantial fines. Any policy or contract on your site still citing "NDPR 2019" is out of date.
