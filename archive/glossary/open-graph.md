# Open Graph

Hidden tags that control the title, description and image shown when your link is shared on WhatsApp, LinkedIn or Facebook.

## Why it matters

Without them a shared link looks broken: no image, and whatever text the platform guesses at. In a market where business is shared through WhatsApp constantly, this small technical detail directly affects whether anyone clicks.
