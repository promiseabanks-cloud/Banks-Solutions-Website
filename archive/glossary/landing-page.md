# Landing Page

A page built for one specific purpose, usually where an advert or campaign sends people.

## Why it matters

It works because it removes choices. A normal page offers a visitor many directions; a landing page offers one. If you are paying for traffic and sending it to your homepage, you are almost certainly wasting a good share of the spend.
