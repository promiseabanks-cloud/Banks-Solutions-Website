# SEO (Search Engine Optimisation)

The work of making your website easier for search engines to find, understand and recommend.

## Why it matters

Modern SEO is mostly unglamorous: a fast site, a clear structure, honest page titles, and pages that genuinely answer the question they claim to. The tricks stopped working years ago. In Nigeria, Google still handles the overwhelming majority of searches, so it remains a large lever, but AI assistants are now a second audience with their own rules.
