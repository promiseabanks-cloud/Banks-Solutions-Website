# Zero-Click Search

A search that gets answered on the results page itself, or by an AI assistant, so the person never visits a website.

## Why it matters

This is the biggest change in how businesses get found. People increasingly ask ChatGPT, Claude or Google's AI answers to recommend someone rather than working through a list of links. Those systems name a handful of businesses, not a hundred, and they can only recommend you if they can read your site and understand plainly what you do and who you serve.
