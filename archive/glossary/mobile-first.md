# Mobile-First

Designing for the phone screen first, then adapting upward to larger screens.

## Why it matters

Over 80% of Nigerian internet users browse on mobile, and Google judges your site primarily on its mobile version. "Mobile-first" means genuinely designing for the phone, not shrinking a desktop layout and hoping. If your site is only ever checked on a laptop, most of your visitors are seeing something you have never looked at.
