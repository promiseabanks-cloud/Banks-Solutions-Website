# Page Speed

How quickly your pages become usable, particularly on a phone using mobile data.

## Why it matters

Speed affects both enquiries and rankings, and slow sites lose visitors before anything is even read. The usual culprits are oversized images, too many plugins, cheap hosting, and loading a dozen font families when two would do. It is one of the most fixable problems there is.
