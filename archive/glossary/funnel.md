# Funnel

The sequence of steps someone passes through from first hearing about you to becoming a customer.

## Why it matters

Useful because it shows you where people drop out. Most businesses assume they have a traffic problem when they actually have a leak at one specific step, often between "interested" and "made contact". Fixing the leak is nearly always cheaper than buying more visitors.
