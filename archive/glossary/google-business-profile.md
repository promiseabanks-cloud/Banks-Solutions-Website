# Google Business Profile

The free business listing that appears in Google Maps and in local search results.

## Why it matters

For any business serving a specific area this is one of the highest-value free things available, and it is frequently neglected. A complete, accurate profile with real photographs, correct opening hours and genuine reviews often produces more enquiries than the website itself.
