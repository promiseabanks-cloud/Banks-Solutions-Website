# XML Sitemap

A machine-readable list of every page on your site, submitted to search engines so nothing gets missed.

## Why it matters

It does not improve rankings by itself, but it makes sure your pages are found and re-checked when they change. Most website software generates one automatically. It is worth confirming yours exists and actually lists the pages you care about.
