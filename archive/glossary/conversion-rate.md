# Conversion Rate

The share of visitors who do the thing you wanted: make an enquiry, book a call, or buy.

## Why it matters

It is the number that decides whether traffic is worth anything. Doubling your conversion rate has exactly the same effect as doubling your visitors, and is usually far cheaper. When a website "is not working", this is almost always the number being described.
