# WordPress

The most widely used website software in the world, powering a large share of all websites.

## Why it matters

Its practical advantage is that you are not locked in, any competent developer can take over a WordPress site, which is not true of proprietary platforms. Its weakness is that it needs maintaining: out-of-date plugins are the most common way small business websites get compromised.
