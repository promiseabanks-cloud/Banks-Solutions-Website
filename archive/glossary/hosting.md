# Hosting

The server space your website lives on, rented monthly or annually.

## Why it matters

Cheap hosting is the most common hidden cause of a slow website, and speed affects both what visitors do and how Google ranks you. Since most Nigerian visitors are on mobile data, a slow server costs you real enquiries. Keep the account in your own name.
