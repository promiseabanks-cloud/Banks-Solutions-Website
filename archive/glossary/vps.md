# VPS (Virtual Private Server)

A hosting arrangement giving you a dedicated slice of a server, rather than sharing one with hundreds of other sites.

## Why it matters

It matters when your site outgrows cheap shared hosting, usually showing up as slowness at busy times, because other sites on the same server are consuming the resources. A VPS costs more and needs someone to manage it, so it is worth it once the website is genuinely producing business.
