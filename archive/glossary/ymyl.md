# YMYL (Your Money or Your Life)

Google's term for content that could affect someone's health, finances, safety or legal position, which is held to a higher standard of trust.

## Why it matters

If you are in finance, law, healthcare or anything similar, this applies to you. It means visible credentials, named authors, real contact details and accurate information matter more for your rankings than for an ordinary business, anonymous content in these fields struggles.
