# Traffic

The number of people visiting your website, and where they came from.

## Why it matters

The least useful number to obsess over. Traffic that never converts is a cost, not an achievement. Look at which sources produce actual enquiries rather than at the headline figure. It is common for a small trickle from search to outperform a large volume from social media.
