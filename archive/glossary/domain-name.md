# Domain Name

Your website address, bankssolution.ng, for example. You rent it annually from a registrar.

## Why it matters

Make sure it is registered in your business name and that you hold the login. Businesses regularly find they cannot move their website because a former developer registered the domain personally. Set it to auto-renew: expired domains are bought within hours by people who then sell them back at a premium.
