# AI (Artificial Intelligence)

Software that performs tasks normally needing human judgement: writing a draft, summarising a document, sorting enquiries, or answering a question.

## Why it matters

For most businesses the useful question is not "should we use AI" but "which repetitive task should it take over first". It earns its money on work that needs a little judgement but not much, and where a person still checks the result. It is a poor choice for anything carrying legal, financial or relationship accountability.
