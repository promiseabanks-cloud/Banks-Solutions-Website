THE PROBLEM

## Where AI genuinely helps.

The test is simple: repetitive work that needs a little judgement, but not much, and where a person still checks the output before it matters.

**You answer the same twenty questions every week.** Different customers, same questions, every time.

**Enquiries need sorting before anyone can act.** Reading each one to decide urgency and who handles it.

**You write the same document repeatedly.** A proposal, a report, a summary: same shape, different details.

**Information is buried in messages.** Months of WhatsApp and email holding answers nobody can find.

**Customers wait overnight for simple answers.** Straightforward questions arriving after hours.

**Someone spends hours moving data between systems.** Copying, reformatting, re-entering.

WHY IT MATTERS

## The honest version, including where it does not help.

AI is very good at a narrow set of things: producing a first draft, summarising a lot of text, sorting and classifying, and answering questions when the answers already exist somewhere. For work of that shape it is genuinely transformative, and it is now cheap enough that small businesses can use it.

It is bad at anything requiring accountability. Final decisions, pricing, client relationships, anything with legal or financial consequence. It is confidently wrong often enough that unchecked output is a real risk. AI drafts. A person still signs.

And there is a sequencing problem nobody selling AI wants to mention: automating a broken process just breaks it faster. If your enquiries are not being captured properly, an AI assistant will not save you: a working CRM will, for less money. We will say that even when it means recommending the smaller job.

We also use AI heavily to run Banks Solution itself: research, drafting, build work, project management. That is not a marketing line; it is the reason one focused operator can deliver at the pace of a much larger team, and it is why we can talk about this honestly rather than theoretically.

The short version

If someone is selling you AI without first asking what your process looks like, they are selling you software, not a solution.
