THE FRONT DOOR

## Everything starts with a diagnosis.

Not a proposal, not a sales call, and not a free review that turns into a pitch.

Most firms in this industry begin with a proposal, because a proposal is a sales document. It assumes the answer before anyone has established the question, and the answer is always whatever that firm most wants to sell.

We begin with a Digital Systems Audit. We look at how your business actually runs, where enquiries come from, where they go, what is manual, what is breaking, and what that is costing you. At the end you have a diagnosis and a defined path, whether or not you work with us.

The diagnosis decides the path. Not your preference, and not our commercial interest. Sometimes it concludes you need less than you thought, and occasionally that you need nothing from us at all.
