## Four entries worth reading first.

S

## CRM (Customer Relationship Management)

A single system holding every customer, enquiry and conversation, so nothing depends on someone remembering.

M

## SEO (Search Engine Optimisation)

The work of making your website easier for search engines to find, understand and recommend.

A

## Zero-Click Search

A search that gets answered on the results page itself, or by an AI assistant, so the person never visits a website.

C

## NDPA 2023 (Nigeria Data Protection Act)

Nigeria's data protection law, in force since June 2023. It replaced the older NDPR 2019 and created the Nigeria Data Protection Commission as regulator.
